Minecraft Content Alumni is an upcoming film database for Minecraft productions: films, series, animations and documentaries made in Minecraft, with pages for each production, its creators and studios, plus ratings, reviews and watchlists.

This is a pre-launch page, not the real site. It's a plain working list of the productions being collected before launch. The look here is deliberately basic and does not reflect the design of the finished website.

Know a Minecraft production that's on IMDb? Submit it below. It's added automatically from IMDb's official data and will be part of the database when the site launches.

Submit a title

Paste IMDb links (one per line, up to 10). You'll confirm on GitHub (free account needed), where you can also attach a poster and synopsis. Titles are added within a few minutes; brand-new IMDb pages can take a day or two to show up in IMDb's data and are retried daily.
